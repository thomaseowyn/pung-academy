Why Pung Academy

The hard part is not learning. It is knowing what to learn.

There has never been more free material for beginners, and that is exactly the problem. Ten tutorials teach the same first lesson, none of them agree on the second, and no one tells you when you are ready to move forward.

Learning without a path

  • Dozens of open tabs, three unfinished courses and no idea which one matters.
  • Jumping between languages before the fundamentals ever settle.
  • Copying code that works without understanding why it works.
  • Progress that feels invisible, which is when most beginners quit.

Learning with Pung Academy

  • One ordered sequence, so there is always exactly one next step.
  • Fundamentals taught before frameworks, in a deliberate order.
  • A short, reviewed list of resources for each stage instead of an endless feed.
  • Visible checkpoints that show how far you have actually come.

How it works

Three steps, in order

You never have to design your own curriculum. Follow the sequence and let each stage unlock the next.

  1. Follow the roadmap

    Each stage lists what to learn, why it matters now, and what you should be able to do before moving on.

  2. Learn from curated resources

    Every topic points to a small, reviewed set of readings, videos and exercises. No infinite lists, no paid detours.

  3. Build toward a career goal

    Once the fundamentals are solid, the roadmap branches into practical directions such as web, data or mobile development.

Resources

Meaningful Materials, not just infinite lists

Every stage of the roadmap will link to a handful of resources that our team has actually carefully and thoroughly reviewed. The library is still being assembled — here is the shape it will take.

Reading

Doing
