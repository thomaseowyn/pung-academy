Why Pung Academy

The hard part is not learning. It is knowing what to learn.

There has never been more free material for beginners, and that is exactly the problem. Ten tutorials teach the same first lesson, none of them agree on the second, and no one tells you when you are ready to move forward.

Learning without a path

  • Dozens of open tabs, three unfinished courses and no idea which one matters.
  • Jumping between languages before the fundamentals ever settle.
  • Copying code that works without understanding why it works.
  • Progress that feels invisible, which is when most beginners quit.

Learning with Pung Academy

  • One ordered sequence, so there is always exactly one next step.
  • Fundamentals taught before frameworks, in a deliberate order.
  • A short, reviewed list of resources for each stage instead of an endless feed.
  • Visible checkpoints that show how far you have actually come.

How it works

Four steps, in order

You never have to design your own curriculum. Start where you are, follow the sequence, and let each stage unlock the next.

  1. Choose your starting point

    A short set of questions places you at the right stage — whether you have never written code or already recognise a for-loop.

  2. Follow the roadmap

    Each stage lists what to learn, why it matters now, and what you should be able to do before moving on.

  3. Learn from curated resources

    Every topic points to a small, reviewed set of readings, videos and exercises. No infinite lists, no paid detours.

  4. Build toward a career goal

    Once the fundamentals are solid, the roadmap branches into practical directions such as web, data or mobile development.

Our learning philosophy

Fundamentals first. Everything else is built on top.

Pung Academy is designed for beginners, not for experienced developers picking up a new tool. That single decision shapes everything: we would rather you understand one concept completely than recognise ten of them vaguely.

Frameworks change every few years. The way a program stores data, repeats work and fails loudly does not. We teach the durable part first, then let you apply it in the direction you care about.

Fundamentals

Logic, data, control flow and how to read an error message without panicking. Slow here means fast later.

Practice

Short, repeated exercises that turn a concept you have read about into something your hands can do without hesitating.

Projects

Real, finishable builds. A project you completed teaches more than a course you abandoned at sixty percent.

Career direction

With the basics secure, you choose a specialisation on evidence rather than on whichever job title sounded best online.
