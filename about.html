About us

We built the guide we wish we had on day one.

Pung Academy is made by four students who each started learning to program without a map. We wasted months on the wrong things, and we think that part is avoidable.

Our purpose

Every one of us began the same way: a search for "how to learn programming", a hundred confident answers, and no way to tell which one to trust. Some of us started with a language that was far too advanced. Some of us finished three introductory courses that all covered the same first week. All of us nearly gave up at a point that had nothing to do with how difficult programming actually is.

Pung Academy exists so that a beginner's first decision is not the hardest one they will make. We are not trying to be the largest catalogue of lessons. We are trying to be the one that puts a small number of good lessons in a defensible order, and then tells you honestly when you are ready for the next one.

Our vision

We want a beginner anywhere to be able to open one page, see exactly where they stand, and know what to do this week. Over time the roadmap will branch into practical career directions — web, data, mobile, software engineering — but the trunk stays the same: fundamentals, practice, projects, direction.

What we hold to

  • Order over volume A short sequence you can finish beats a huge library you cannot navigate.
  • Understanding over copying If you cannot explain why the code works, the lesson is not finished.
  • Honesty about difficulty We say when a stage is hard, rather than promising results in thirty days.
  • Beginners first Every decision is judged by whether it helps someone on their very first week.
