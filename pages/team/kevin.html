About

How I got here

I did not start with programming. I started with a spreadsheet that had grown far too large for anyone to maintain by hand, and a stubborn refusal to keep copying rows between tabs every Friday afternoon. Someone told me a short script could do it. That script took me two weeks, broke constantly, and was almost certainly the worst code I have ever written — but it ran, and it saved me an hour a week, and I have not really stopped since.

What followed was the part I now try to spare other people. I learned three languages badly before I learned one properly. I bounced between a video course, a book, and a documentation site that all assumed I had already read the other two. For nearly six months I was busy every single day and could not have told you whether I was actually getting better. The turning point was not a better tutorial; it was finally being told, by a patient senior student, that I should stop starting new things and finish one small thing instead.

What I work on at Pung Academy

I own the sequence: which topic comes first, what has to be understood before the next stage opens, and where the honest checkpoints sit. It is less glamorous than building features, but the order is the product. A brilliant lesson delivered three weeks too early is just a discouraging lesson. I also handle the data side of our roadmaps — how a path is described, stored, and eventually branched into career directions once the fundamentals are solid.

Interests

Backend systems, databases, and the unreasonable satisfaction of a query that finally returns in milliseconds. Away from a screen I play badminton badly and cook the same three dishes very well.

Goals

To become a backend engineer who is trusted with the boring, critical parts of a system, and to keep teaching on the side — because explaining something is still the fastest way I have found to discover what I do not understand.
