Bryan Vegard Suwarno

"Guided learning is the key to learning."

Pung Academy Frontend Course Development

My journey into technology began when I built my first automation using n8n. I was amazed by how advanced AI had become. For the first time, technology felt less like a tool I was learning to use and more like a force that could turn almost any idea into reality. My first project was for my mom's business, where I automated administrative tasks such as recording food expenses into an Excel spreadsheet. What had previously been a bottleneck—whether because the admin's internet or computer was down, or for other operational reasons—could suddenly be handled automatically. With just an AI API and a server, the process could run reliably without depending on a person being available.

That experience completely changed how I saw technology. I realized how far AI had progressed, while many people and businesses were still working as if these technologies did not exist. It made me realize that the gap was no longer about what technology could do, but how quickly people could adapt and put it to use. That inspired me to become someone who can bridge that gap—bringing powerful technologies to more people, helping them integrate AI into their work and lives, and ultimately using technology to create meaningful impact.

AI is going to change the world, and I want to be part of that change.

What I do at Pung Academy

I work on both the frontend and course development, turning our learning roadmap into a clear, engaging, and easy-to-navigate experience. I review each resource to make sure it fits naturally into the learning path, while designing the course structure and interface to keep it visually appealing and packed with useful information.

My goal is to make learning feel straightforward: students should know where to go, what to learn next, and why each part of the course matters—without getting lost in unnecessary complexity.

Where I am heading

I want to work in Applied AI for Finance, specifically in algorithmic trading—building systems that combine machine learning, data, and financial markets to make smarter, faster, and more systematic trading decisions. I am particularly interested in developing quantitative strategies and turning complex market data into actionable insights through technology.
